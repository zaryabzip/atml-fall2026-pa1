Discriminator learning-rate change for DANN and CDAN -- recorded 2026-09-24, BEFORE the new runs were trained.

HONEST CONTEXT: the Sketch (target) results of the previous DANN/CDAN runs HAD ALREADY BEEN SEEN when this was decided
(DANN 29.7%, CDAN 78.3% Sketch accuracy). Those results are kept in task2/results/_backup_disc_lr1x/.

Change: the domain discriminator gets 10x the learning rate of the rest of the model (1e-3 instead of 1e-4;
method.disc_lr_mult: 10). Same change for DANN and CDAN, because the handout sets CDAN's discriminator up like DANN's.
Everything else unchanged: discriminator shape, dropout, alpha schedule, loss weight, disc_input_norm fix, seed.

Reason (training-side evidence only, no Sketch labels): in the previous DANN run the discriminator's epoch-mean accuracy
stayed far BELOW chance (0.16-0.36) for the whole run. A discriminator that kept up with the backbone would simply
flip its answers and be right again, so being stuck below 50% means it adapts too slowly; the backbone was flipping the
domain signal instead of removing it. A faster discriminator is the standard remedy (DANN codebases give the newly
initialized discriminator a higher learning rate than the pretrained backbone).

Pass/fail rule, fixed now, checked WITHOUT any Sketch labels:
  1. Discriminator epoch-mean accuracy over the second half of training within about 0.45-0.65, and domain loss near
     ln 2 = 0.69 (the discriminator is confused, not fooled into flipping).
  2. Best mean source-validation macro-F1 no more than ~0.02 below the previous run (DANN 0.947, CDAN 0.940).
  3. Domain separability (source-val vs unlabeled Sketch features; no labels) below the previous run's
     (DANN 0.999, CDAN 0.982).
Decision: the new runs replace the old ones as the main Task 2 DANN/CDAN results only if they pass 1 and 2 (3 is
reported either way). Otherwise the previous runs stay the main results and these are reported as a documented
extra attempt. Sketch accuracy plays no part in this decision, whatever it turns out to be.
